# Chapter 5 — The Other Way Around

*Opposite categories, duality, yin and yang*

Given:

\[
A\longrightarrow B,
\]

category theory permits us to reverse the arrows:

\[
A\longleftarrow B.
\]

An entire coherent category has an opposite:

\[
\mathcal C^{op}.
\]

Products become coproducts.

Initial becomes terminal.

Other constructions acquire duals.

The temptation to say simply

\[
\mathcal C=\text{yang},
\qquad
\mathcal C^{op}=\text{yin}
\]

must be resisted.

The deeper correspondence is that **reversing relational direction can reveal another coherent perspective rather than destroying the first**.
