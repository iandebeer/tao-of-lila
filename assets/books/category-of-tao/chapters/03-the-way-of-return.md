# Chapter 3 — The Way of Return

*Isomorphism, sameness, difference, return*

\[
A\xrightarrow{f}B\xrightarrow{f^{-1}}A
\]

Returning is not necessarily the same as never having departed.

Chapter 40:

**Returning is the movement of Dao.**

The categorical distinction between equality and isomorphism becomes philosophically important:

\[
A=B
\]

is not the same statement as

\[
A\cong B.
\]

Something may undergo transformation and yet preserve structure.

The traveller returns.

The shore remains.

The traveller does not.

The moon remains undisturbed while its reflection changes with the water.

This chapter begins asking:

**What does it mean to remain oneself through transformation?**

That question carries us from isomorphism toward identity.
