# Chapter 8 — Ways That Agree

*Composition, commutative diagrams, coherence*

Different paths can lead from \(A\) to \(C\):

\[
A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C
\]

and

\[
A\rightarrow D\rightarrow C.
\]

When those paths amount to the same morphism, the diagram commutes.

Water returns as the central image.

There may be many ways down the mountain.

The important question is not whether every stream follows the same route but whether the relationships cohere.
