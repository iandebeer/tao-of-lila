# Chapter 7 — Form and Emptiness

*Śūnyatā, relation, universal characterization*

Here the *Heart Sutra* enters more fully.

**Form is emptiness.  
Emptiness is form.**

Emptiness is not treated as:

\[
0
\]

nor as

\[
\varnothing.
\]

Śūnyatā does not mean simple nonexistence.

The categorical correspondence is instead relational:

> **Emptiness does not remove the object. It removes the illusion that the object stands alone.**

Instead of searching for an isolated essence inside \(A\), consider:

\[
\cdots\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow\cdots
\]

Form remains.

Difference remains.

Relationship becomes constitutive of how the thing can be understood.

This prepares the reader for Yoneda without yet naming its mathematics in detail.
