# Chapter 4 — The One and the Many

*Products, coproducts, universal properties*

Chapter 42 provides the natural Taoist ground:

**Dao gives birth to One.  
One gives birth to Two.  
Two gives birth to Three.  
Three gives birth to the ten thousand things.**

Products and coproducts introduce the reader to **universal properties**.

This is a major conceptual step.

An object need not always be described by opening it and listing what it contains.

It may instead be characterized by the uniqueness of its relationships.

This begins moving the book from:

**What is it?**

toward:

**How does it relate?**
