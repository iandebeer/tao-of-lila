# Chapter 8 — Between Worlds

*Functors*

A functor carries one categorical structure into another while respecting identities and composition.

This becomes a language for translation.

Not identity.

Not reduction.

Not saying Taoism is Buddhism, Buddhism is Advaita, or mathematics is spirituality.

Rather:

**What structure survives translation?**

This chapter can also explain the method of the book itself.

We are repeatedly attempting imperfect translations between conceptual worlds.
