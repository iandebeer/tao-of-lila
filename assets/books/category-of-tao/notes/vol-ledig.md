# Vol-ledig

Working material for Chapter 12 (Emptiness) and for the book's bilingual
convention. Promote into the chapter when ready; keep the Afrikaans primary.

## Poem

### Vol-ledig

Die nut van die pot is in die holte van die klei.
Die spasie tussen mure maak die huis waarin ek bly.
Die skip beweeg weens seile wat bol in die wind.
In die leegheid van self, is waar ek die heelheid weer vind.

### Plain English handrail

The usefulness of the pot is in the hollow of the clay.
The space between walls makes the house in which I live.
The ship moves because of sails that belly in the wind.
In the emptiness of self is where I find wholeness again.

This is a handrail, not a second poem competing with the original.

## Linguistic hinge

*Volledig* is the ordinary Afrikaans word for “complete” or “whole.” Broken
open as *vol-ledig*: *vol*, full; *ledig*, empty.

The translation therefore cannot quite follow.

- “Complete” gives the conventional meaning of the word, but loses the paradox
  through which the meaning came.
- “Full-empty” preserves the components but sounds unnatural.
- “Emptied into fullness” captures some philosophy but ceases to be a
  translation.

None is wrong; none is *Vol-ledig*.

That failure is philosophically meaningful. The name cannot exhaust what is
named.

## Categorical sketch

\[
\text{Afrikaans experience}
\longrightarrow
\text{English translation}
\]

but:

\[
\text{translation}\neq\text{experience}.
\]

Afrikaans and English need not be competing containers for identical content.
They can be different domains connected by mappings that preserve some
structures while necessarily transforming others.

Something survives the morphism, and something does not.

## Resonance

- *Tao Te Ching* 11: the usefulness of the pot, the house, the spokes — in the
  empty.
- The book's bilingual principle: Afrikaans where the insight originated;
  English as carrying-across, not replacement.
- Emptiness that is not nihilism: the hollow that makes the vessel useful;
  the self emptied so that wholeness can return.
