# Earlier architecture and drafts

These files preserve the manuscript as it stood before the four-part,
fourteen-chapter working architecture was adopted, including the local edits
to `02-change.md` and `outline.md`. They are source material, outside the
current reading order in [the outline](../../outline.md).

- `01-the-monad-and-the-tao.md`: retain the container/context exploration as
  material for Chapters 6, 10 and 12; monads now follow functors and natural
  transformations rather than opening the book.
- `02-change.md`: the complete earlier identity draft. Its poem, English
  rendering and prose are carried into Chapter 3, with the new exploration
  of diminishing predicates, neti neti and Sat–Chit–Ānanda added.
- `03-relationships.md` and `09-category-theory.md`: seeds for the relational
  progression through the new chapters.
- `04-yin-yang.md`: material for Chapter 5, with categorical duality kept
  distinct from yin and yang.
- `05-trigrams.md` through `08-the-oracle.md`: I Ching material retained for
  later supporting examples or appendices; these are no longer dedicated
  chapters in the main sequence.
- `10-the-category-of-tao.md`: earlier synthesis questions, available to
  Chapters 12–14 without replacing their new culmination.
- `outline.md` and `concept-graph.md`: the previous planning documents.

The new chapter files other than Chapter 3 contain the supplied working
passages and development directions. The opening text of *Anderkant nêrens*
is pending; it has not been invented. The glossary follows the supplied
working glossary. Quotations and translation choices still require source
attribution before publication.
